Ripple app screenshots
======================

Drop real screenshots from the Ripple app into this folder and they appear in
the case study automatically, inside phone frames. No code change needed.

Naming
------
The filename becomes the caption. A leading number controls the order and is
stripped from the caption:

    01-event-discovery.png   ->  "Event discovery"
    02-map.png               ->  "Map"
    03-ticket-checkout.png   ->  "Ticket checkout"

Accepted extensions: .png .jpg .jpeg .webp .avif

Guidance
--------
- Portrait screenshots, roughly 9:19.5 (a normal phone screengrab).
- Three to five is plenty. They scroll horizontally.
- Keep each file under ~300 KB; images are served as-is, not re-compressed.
- With no files here, the section does not render at all — the site never
  shows a placeholder or a mocked-up screen.
